# Studying in the USA — Fees, Scholarships, and Budgeting

The financial aspect of studying in the US is one of the most critical factors for Nepali families. Tuition and living expenses can vary widely depending on the institution type, location, and scholarship awards. As of June 2026, careful financial planning and exploring funding options are essential steps for prospective students [Source: EducationUSA — https://educationusa.state.gov].

## Tuition Fees by Institution Type

Annual tuition costs vary significantly:

- **Community Colleges:** $5,000 to $12,000 per year.
- **Public Universities (Out-of-State):** $15,000 to $35,000 per year.
- **Private Universities / Liberal Arts Colleges:** $35,000 to $65,000+ per year.

## Cost of Living

Living costs depend heavily on the location (urban vs. rural) and lifestyle. On average, students should budget between $10,000 and $18,000 per year:
- **On-Campus Housing (Dorms and Meal Plans):** $9,000 to $15,000 per year.
- **Off-Campus Housing (Shared apartments):** $500 to $1,200 per month (plus utilities).
- **Books, Insurance, and Personal Expenses:** $2,500 to $4,000 per year.

## Scholarships and Financial Aid

US universities offer several financial aid options for international students:
- **Merit-Based Scholarships:** Awarded based on academic excellence, high GPA, and strong SAT/ACT scores. These can range from $2,000 to full tuition.
- **Need-Based Financial Aid:** Awarded based on family financial need. Students must submit the CSS Profile (College Scholarship Service) or the ISFAA (International Student Financial Aid Application). Some elite colleges are "need-blind" for international students.
- **Graduate Assistantships:** Graduate students can apply for Teaching Assistantships (TA) or Research Assistantships (RA), which typically waive tuition fees and provide a monthly living stipend in exchange for 15-20 hours of work per week.

## Initial Setup Costs in Nepal

Before arriving in the US, Nepali students must budget for:
- SEVIS I-901 Fee: $350 [Source: Department of Homeland Security — https://studyinthestates.dhs.gov].
- F-1 Visa Application Fee: $185 [Source: US Department of State — https://travel.state.gov].
- Standardized test fees (SAT, TOEFL, IELTS, Duolingo).
- Academic document translation, evaluation (WES), and courier fees.
- Airfare: $1,000 to $1,800.


## Additional Detailed RAG Reference and Q&A

This section provides supplementary reference material, detailed questions, and official guidelines to assist in search retrieval:

- **Question:** What is the primary official portal for updates?
  **Answer:** As of June 2026, students must monitor the official website to ensure all regulations, application procedures, and visa rules are followed. For official updates, refer to travel.state.gov for the USA and migri.fi for Finland. These sites are regularly updated with policy adjustments and fee schedules.
- **Question:** How do Nepali students apply for their NOC?
  **Answer:** Nepali students must apply online via the Ministry of Education, Science and Technology (MOEST) portal. The application requires a valid admission offer, proof of passport, and citizenship. The processing fee is NPR 2,000, and once approved, the NOC is required to exchange foreign currency and complete the visa process.
- **Question:** What is the role of sponsors in the application process?
  **Answer:** Sponsors provide the financial guarantee necessary to cover tuition fees, health insurance, and living expenses. In the USA, sponsors can be parents, relatives, or self-funded via bank loans. In Finland, sponsors must transfer funds to the student's personal bank account, as Migri does not accept third-party sponsor letters directly.
