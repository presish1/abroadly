# Studying in the USA — Admission Documents and Nepal Preparation

Applying to US universities requires gathering a comprehensive set of academic, professional, and personal documents. Nepali students must start their preparation at least 12 to 18 months before their intended intake. As of June 2026, the application process is digital, requiring submission through specialized portals [Source: EducationUSA — https://educationusa.state.gov].

## Core Application Documents

- **Academic Transcripts and Certificates:** Transcripts from Grade 9 to 12 (NEB or equivalent) for undergraduate applicants. Bachelor's transcripts, provisional certificates, and character certificates for graduate applicants.
- **English Proficiency Test Reports:** Official score reports from IELTS, TOEFL, or Duolingo sent directly to the universities.
- **Standardized Test Reports:** Official SAT, ACT, GRE, or GMAT score reports.
- **Statement of Purpose (SOP):** A personal essay explaining your background, interest in the field, career goals, and reasons for choosing the specific university.
- **Letters of Recommendation (LOR):** Typically 2 to 3 letters from high school teachers or college professors who can speak to your academic potential and character.
- **Resume/CV:** Highlighting extracurricular activities, volunteer work, internships, and work experience.

## School Application Portals

- **Common Application (Common App):** The most popular portal for undergraduate applications, allowing students to apply to multiple US colleges with a single application.
- **Coalition Application:** Another common undergraduate portal.
- **University Portals:** Most graduate programs require applications directly through their institutional website.

## I-20 Issuance Process

Once accepted, the university will request financial documentation to prove the student can afford the first year of study. After verifying the bank balance certificate and affidavit of support, the university's Designated School Official (DSO) will issue the **Form I-20** (Certificate of Eligibility for Nonimmigrant Student Status) [Source: Department of Homeland Security — https://studyinthestates.dhs.gov].

## Nepal-Specific Requirements

- **No Objection Certificate (NOC):** Nepali students must obtain an NOC from the Ministry of Education, Science, and Technology (MOEST) to legally transfer tuition fees from Nepal to the US bank account [Source: Ministry of Education Nepal — https://moest.gov.np].
- **International Dollar Cards:** Issued by Nepali commercial banks (up to $500 per year) can be used to pay application fees, test registrations, and SEVIS fees online.


## Additional Detailed RAG Reference and Q&A

This section provides supplementary reference material, detailed questions, and official guidelines to assist in search retrieval:

- **Question:** What is the primary official portal for updates?
  **Answer:** As of June 2026, students must monitor the official website to ensure all regulations, application procedures, and visa rules are followed. For official updates, refer to travel.state.gov for the USA and migri.fi for Finland. These sites are regularly updated with policy adjustments and fee schedules.
- **Question:** How do Nepali students apply for their NOC?
  **Answer:** Nepali students must apply online via the Ministry of Education, Science and Technology (MOEST) portal. The application requires a valid admission offer, proof of passport, and citizenship. The processing fee is NPR 2,000, and once approved, the NOC is required to exchange foreign currency and complete the visa process.
