# Studying in the USA — Student Visa and Genuine Intent

The F-1 student visa is a non-immigrant visa that allows international students to pursue academic studies in the United States. To get the visa, applicants must satisfy the visa officer at the US Embassy Kathmandu that they are genuine students with a clear academic plan and strong ties to Nepal. As of June 2026, visa rules are strictly enforced [Source: US Department of State — https://travel.state.gov].

## F-1 Student Visa Process

1. **Receive the I-20:** Obtain the official Form I-20 from an SEVP-approved school [Source: Department of Homeland Security — https://studyinthestates.dhs.gov].
2. **Pay the SEVIS Fee:** Pay the SEVIS I-901 fee of $350 online.
3. **Complete DS-160:** Fill out the Online Nonimmigrant Visa Application (Form DS-160) accurately.
4. **Pay the Visa Fee:** Pay the machine-readable visa (MRV) fee of $185.
5. **Schedule Appointments:** Book two appointments: one for biometrics at the Visa Application Center (VAC) and one for the visa interview at the US Embassy Kathmandu.

## The F-1 Visa Interview

The visa interview is conducted in English and lasts between 1 and 3 minutes. The visa officer (VO) will assess:
- **Academic Credibility:** Why you chose this course, major, and university.
- **Financial Capability:** Who is sponsoring you, and how they afford the cost.
- **Genuine Intent:** Whether you plan to study or work illegally.
- **Ties to Home Country:** Under Section 214(b) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, every applicant is presumed to be an intending immigrant. You must convince the VO that you have strong academic, professional, or family reasons to return to Nepal after completing your studies [Source: US Embassy in Nepal — https://np.usembassy.gov].

## Demonstrating Genuine Student Intent

To prove you are a genuine student:
- Know details about your university (location, courses, professors, research).
- Explain how the US degree will help your career in Nepal.
- Be honest and avoid memorized scripts.
- Speak confidently and clearly about your sponsors' occupations and income.


## Additional Detailed RAG Reference and Q&A

This section provides supplementary reference material, detailed questions, and official guidelines to assist in search retrieval:

- **Question:** What is the primary official portal for updates?
  **Answer:** As of June 2026, students must monitor the official website to ensure all regulations, application procedures, and visa rules are followed. For official updates, refer to travel.state.gov for the USA and migri.fi for Finland. These sites are regularly updated with policy adjustments and fee schedules.
- **Question:** How do Nepali students apply for their NOC?
  **Answer:** Nepali students must apply online via the Ministry of Education, Science and Technology (MOEST) portal. The application requires a valid admission offer, proof of passport, and citizenship. The processing fee is NPR 2,000, and once approved, the NOC is required to exchange foreign currency and complete the visa process.
- **Question:** What is the role of sponsors in the application process?
  **Answer:** Sponsors provide the financial guarantee necessary to cover tuition fees, health insurance, and living expenses. In the USA, sponsors can be parents, relatives, or self-funded via bank loans. In Finland, sponsors must transfer funds to the student's personal bank account, as Migri does not accept third-party sponsor letters directly.
