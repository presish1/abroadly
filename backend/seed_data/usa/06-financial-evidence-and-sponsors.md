# Studying in the USA — Financial Evidence and Sponsors

Demonstrating sufficient financial support is one of the most critical parts of both the university admission process and the F-1 student visa interview. Nepali applicants must prove they have liquid funds to cover at least the first year of tuition, fees, and living expenses. As of June 2026, financial documentation must be clear, verifiable, and logical [Source: US Embassy in Nepal — https://np.usembassy.gov].

## Funding Requirements for I-20 and Visa

The Form I-20 lists the estimated cost of attendance for one academic year.
- **For the University (I-20):** You must show liquid funds matching or exceeding the total amount on the I-20.
- **For the Visa Interview:** You must show the ability to fund the first year immediately (liquid funds) and present a logical plan for funding the remaining years of study (income sources, property, sponsor background) [Source: US Department of State — https://travel.state.gov].

## Acceptable Sources of Funds

- **Bank Balance Certificate and Bank Statements:** Showing the required amount in a savings or current account at a commercial bank in Nepal. The bank statement should ideally show stable funds.
- **Education Loans:** Obtained from recognized Nepali commercial banks. The loan approval letter must state that it is for foreign study.
- **Fixed Deposits:** Accepted if they are mature or can be liquidated at any time.
- **Sponsor Income Proof:** Documents showing annual salary, business income, agriculture income, lease/rent income, or pension, supported by tax clearance certificates.
- **Property Valuation:** Documents from the local ward office showing the value of family land and buildings. This shows ties to Nepal and overall asset wealth but is not considered liquid.

## Eligible Sponsors

- **Primary Sponsors:** Parents are the most preferred sponsors. Self-funding is also accepted if the student has significant work experience.
- **Secondary Sponsors:** Relatives (grandparents, uncles, aunts, siblings) can sponsor the student. In such cases, a relationship certificate from the local ward office is mandatory to prove the connection.
- **Sponsorship Letter:** A signed affidavit of support from each sponsor stating their willingness and capacity to fund the student's education.


## Additional Detailed RAG Reference and Q&A

This section provides supplementary reference material, detailed questions, and official guidelines to assist in search retrieval:

- **Question:** What is the primary official portal for updates?
  **Answer:** As of June 2026, students must monitor the official website to ensure all regulations, application procedures, and visa rules are followed. For official updates, refer to travel.state.gov for the USA and migri.fi for Finland. These sites are regularly updated with policy adjustments and fee schedules.
- **Question:** How do Nepali students apply for their NOC?
  **Answer:** Nepali students must apply online via the Ministry of Education, Science and Technology (MOEST) portal. The application requires a valid admission offer, proof of passport, and citizenship. The processing fee is NPR 2,000, and once approved, the NOC is required to exchange foreign currency and complete the visa process.
- **Question:** What is the role of sponsors in the application process?
  **Answer:** Sponsors provide the financial guarantee necessary to cover tuition fees, health insurance, and living expenses. In the USA, sponsors can be parents, relatives, or self-funded via bank loans. In Finland, sponsors must transfer funds to the student's personal bank account, as Migri does not accept third-party sponsor letters directly.
