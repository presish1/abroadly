# Studying in Ireland — Pathways and Entry Requirements

To apply for admission to Irish higher education, Nepali students must understand how their academic qualifications align with the Irish education system. Admissions are highly transparent but competitive, focusing on academic merit and English language proficiency. As of June 2026, Irish universities require international qualifications to match specific levels on the Irish National Framework of Qualifications [Source: Quality and Qualifications Ireland — https://www.qqi.ie/].

## The National Framework of Qualifications (NFQ)

Ireland uses the **National Framework of Qualifications (NFQ)** to measure and compare the standard of educational qualifications.

- **NFQ Level 7:** Ordinary Bachelor Degree (typically 3 years).
- **NFQ Level 8:** Honours Bachelor Degree (typically 3 to 4 years) or Higher Diploma.
- **NFQ Level 9:** Master's Degree (typically 1 to 2 years) or Postgraduate Diploma.
- **NFQ Level 10:** Doctoral Degree (PhD).

Most Nepali students apply for NFQ Level 8 (Honours Bachelor) or NFQ Level 9 (Master's) programs.

## Undergraduate Entry Requirements (Bachelor's)

Nepali students who have completed their National Examinations Board (NEB) Class 12 or equivalent can apply for bachelor's degrees.

- **Academic Requirements:** A minimum overall average of 65% to 75% in NEB Class 12 (or a GPA of 2.8+ out of 4.0). Specific subjects (like Mathematics for Engineering and Computer Science) may require higher individual scores.
- **Foundation Pathways:** If a student's high school scores do not meet the direct entry threshold, they can enroll in a 1-year International Foundation Year program offered by university partners (such as Study Group or Kaplan) to transition into the degree program.

## Postgraduate Entry Requirements (Master's)

Irish master's programs are typically 1 year in duration (intensive), though some research-based programs take 2 years.

- **Academic Requirements:** A completed bachelor's degree (minimum 3 or 4 years) from a recognized university in Nepal.
- **Grading Equivalence:** Most universities require a First Class degree or a high Second Class Upper division (roughly 60% to 65%+ in Nepal's university system, or a GPA of 3.0+ out of 4.0).
- **Relevant Background:** Irish master's programs are specialized, meaning you must have a matching undergraduate background (e.g., a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science is required for a Master's in Data Analytics).

## English Language Proficiency Requirements

Since English is the medium of instruction, proving language proficiency is mandatory.

| Test | Minimum Score (Bachelor's) | Minimum Score (Master's) |
|---|---|---|
| **IELTS Academic** | 6.0 (no band below 5.5) | 6.5 (no band below 6.0) |
| **TOEFL iBT** | 80 overall (minimum 18 in subscores) | 90 overall (minimum 20 in subscores) |
| **PTE Academic** | 50 to 58 | 58 to 65 |
| **Duolingo English Test (DET)** | 105 to 115 | 115 to 125 |

Some competitive programs (like Journalism, Medicine, or Law) require higher IELTS bands (e.g., 7.0 or 7.5 overall) [Source: Education in Ireland — https://www.educationinireland.com/en/How-Do-I-Apply-/Entry-Requirements/].
