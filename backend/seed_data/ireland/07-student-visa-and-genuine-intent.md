# Studying in Ireland — Student Visa and Genuine Intent

Nepali students require a Study Visa (Category D - Long Stay) to enter Ireland for academic purposes. The visa application process is rigorous, and the visa decision is made by the Irish visa office in New Delhi (which handles applications from the Indian subcontinent). As of June 2026, applications must be submitted online via the AVATS system, followed by document submission at VFS Global in Kathmandu [Source: Irish Immigration Service — https://www.irishimmigration.ie/].

## The Irish Student Visa Application Process

The application process involves several steps that should be completed sequentially:

1. **AVATS Online Application:** Fill out the online visa application form on the AVATS portal, select the correct visa type (Study, Long Stay, Multi-Entry or Single-Entry), and print the application summary sheet.
2. **Paying the Visa Fee:** The standard visa fee is €60 for a single entry and €100 for a multi-entry visa, payable in local currency through VFS Global.
3. **Submit Documents:** Book an appointment at the VFS Global center in Kathmandu to submit your physical application dossier, passport, and biometrics.
4. **Processing Time:** The average processing time is 4 to 8 weeks. Students are advised to apply at least 8 to 12 weeks before their course start date.

## VFS Global Submission and Biometrics in Kathmandu

VFS Global acts as the administrative partner for the Irish visa authorities in Nepal.

- **Biometric Enrolment:** During your VFS appointment, you will undergo biometric enrolment, which includes a digital photograph and a scan of your fingerprints.
- **Physical Submission:** You must present all original documents along with a full set of photocopies. Original documents (such as academic certificates and bank books) will be returned after processing.

## Crucial Visa Documents Checklist

You must submit the following documents in your visa file:

- **Application Summary Sheet:** Signed AVATS printout.
- **Passport:** Valid for at least 12 months, plus previous passports if any.
- **Letter of Acceptance:** Unconditional admission letter from an Irish university confirming enrollment in an ILEP-listed course.
- **Tuition Fee Receipt:** Official receipt showing payment of tuition fees (minimum €6,000, but paying full first-year fees is highly recommended) [Source: Irish Immigration Service — https://www.irishimmigration.ie/coming-to-study-in-ireland/].
- **Financial Evidence:** Bank statements showing at least €10,000 available for living expenses, along with sponsor details.
- **English Language Certificate:** IELTS, PTE, or Duolingo scores.
- **Private Medical Insurance:** Proof of coverage for your stay.
- **Study Gap Explanation:** If you have study gaps, you must provide employment letters or experience certificates to cover the gaps.

## Demonstrating Academic and Genuine Intent

The visa officer will assess your "genuine intent" to study in Ireland and return home. You must convey this through your **Motivation Letter (SOP)**:

- **Academic Integrity:** Explain why you chose this specific course and how it builds upon your past studies or work experience.
- **Why Ireland?** State why you chose Ireland over other options (such as the UK, USA, or Australia) and why you chose this particular institution.
- **Future Career Plans:** Detail the specific job roles you intend to apply for in Nepal or globally after graduation, showing that the Irish degree is a key driver for your career progression.
- **Ties to Nepal:** Highlight your strong familial and economic ties to Nepal, demonstrating your intent to return after your studies.
