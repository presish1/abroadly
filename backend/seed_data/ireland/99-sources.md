# Studying in Ireland — Official Sources and Useful Links

When planning your education in Ireland, it is essential to consult official, authoritative channels to obtain the most accurate information. Unverified information from forums can lead to visa refusals or administrative issues. As of June 2026, the websites listed below are the official, legally recognized resources for academics, visa applications, work permits, and student life in Ireland [Source: Irish Immigration Service — https://www.irishimmigration.ie/].

## Government and Immigration Portals

- **Irish Immigration Service (An tSeirbhís Inimirce):** The primary authority under the Department of Justice responsible for visa policy, entry requirements, IRP registration, and post-study work schemes.
  - *Link:* https://www.irishimmigration.ie/
- **AVATS Online Visa Portal:** The official online system where all international student visa applications must be registered.
  - *Link:* https://www.visas.inis.gov.ie/avats/
- **Department of Enterprise, Trade and Employment (DETE):** The government department overseeing employment regulations, work permits (Critical Skills and General Permits), and eligible occupations lists.
  - *Link:* https://enterprise.gov.ie/en/
- **Department of Justice, Ireland:** The ministry overseeing civil law, immigration, and citizenship processes.
  - *Link:* https://www.justice.ie/

## Academic and Research Databases

- **Education in Ireland:** The national brand under Enterprise Ireland, promoting Irish higher education institutions globally. It offers a comprehensive course finder, scholarship details, and guides for international students.
  - *Link:* https://www.educationinireland.com/
- **Quality and Qualifications Ireland (QQI):** The state agency responsible for quality assurance in education. QQI maintains the National Framework of Qualifications (NFQ) database.
  - *Link:* https://www.qqi.ie/
- **Interim List of Eligible Programmes (ILEP):** The official database listing all courses approved for student visa purposes (Stamp 2 eligibility).
  - *Link:* https://www.irishimmigration.ie/coming-to-study-in-ireland/interim-list-of-eligible-programmes-ilep/
- **Higher Education Authority (HEA):** The statutory planning and funding body for higher education in Ireland, overseeing the GOI-IES scholarship scheme.
  - *Link:* https://hea.ie/

## Student Services and Practical Resources

- **VFS Global Nepal (Ireland Section):** The visa application center in Kathmandu that handles physical document submission and biometric enrolment for Ireland.
  - *Link:* https://www.vfsglobal.com/
- **Student Leap Card:** The official transport discount card for college and university students in Ireland.
  - *Link:* https://www.studentleapcard.ie/
- **Irish Council for International Students (ICOS):** An independent national organisation advocate for the rights and welfare of international students in Ireland.
  - *Link:* https://www.internationalstudents.ie/
