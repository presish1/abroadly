# Studying in Ireland — Health Insurance, Medical, and Biometrics

Nepali students planning to study in Ireland must comply with specific health, medical, and biometric requirements. These measures are designed to ensure safety, verify identity, and provide access to medical care during your stay. As of June 2026, private medical insurance is a mandatory visa requirement, and students must complete immigration registration within 90 days of arrival [Source: Irish Immigration Service — https://www.irishimmigration.ie/].

## Mandatory Medical Insurance for Student Visas

Ireland does not allow international students to access its public healthcare system for free. Therefore, you must arrange for private medical insurance before arriving in the country.

- **Visa Requirement:** Proof of private medical insurance must be submitted as part of your visa application. The policy must cover a minimum of €25,000 for accident and emergency medical treatment, including hospitalisation [Source: Irish Immigration Service — https://www.irishimmigration.ie/coming-to-study-in-ireland/].
- **Coverage Type:**
  - **First-Year Students:** A standard travel medical insurance policy from an approved provider (such as O'Driscoll O'Neil, StudyandProtect, or Allianz) is acceptable.
  - **Subsequent Years:** Once in Ireland, you must renew your health insurance annually using a policy specifically designed for non-EU students residing in Ireland.
- **Group Insurance:** Many Irish universities offer discounted group medical insurance schemes. If your university does, you can pay the insurance fee alongside your tuition and use the university receipt as proof.

## Medical Checks and Health Regulations

Unlike some destinations, Ireland does not require Nepali students to undergo a formal pre-departure medical check-up or obtain a tuberculosis (TB) clearance certificate as part of the visa application process.

- **General Health Advice:** It is highly recommended to have standard vaccinations (MMR, Hepatitis B, Tetanus, and Meningitis C) before departure.
- **Bringing Medication:** If you are bringing prescription medicine from Nepal, you must keep it in its original packaging and carry a doctor's prescription in English stating the medical condition and dosage.

## Biometric Data Collection at VFS Kathmandu

Submitting biometric data is a standard part of the visa application process to verify your identity.

- **What is collected?** You must provide a digital photograph and a scan of your fingerprints.
- **Where is it done?** The biometrics are collected at the VFS Global center in Kathmandu during your physical document submission.
- **Exemptions:** Children under the age of 5 do not require fingerprint scans but must still have their photograph taken.

## Registering with the Garda National Immigration Bureau (GNIB/IRP)

A student visa only grants you permission to enter Ireland. To reside in the country for the duration of your course, you must register with the immigration office.

- **Irish Residence Permit (IRP):** Formerly known as the GNIB card, the IRP is a credit-card-sized document containing your biometrics, photo, and visa status (Stamp 2).
- **Registration Process:**
  - **Appointment:** Book an appointment with the local registration office (in Dublin, this is done online; outside Dublin, you must visit the local Garda station).
  - **Required Documents:** Passport, Letter of Acceptance, proof of health insurance, proof of Irish bank account showing €4,500 (or €3,000 if registered before July 2023), and the €300 registration fee.
  - **Fee:** The IRP card costs €300 and must be renewed annually [Source: Irish Immigration Service — https://www.irishimmigration.ie/registering-your-permission/].
