# Studying in Ireland — Application Timeline and Nepali Student FAQ

A successful application to study in Ireland requires careful coordination. From preparation for English proficiency exams to university admission, NOC collection, fee payment, and visa processing, the entire process takes about 8 to 12 months. As of June 2026, Nepali students are strongly advised to apply early, especially due to high visa processing volumes and housing constraints in major Irish cities [Source: Education in Ireland — https://www.educationinireland.com/].

## The Step-by-Step Ireland Application Timeline

Most Irish universities run on a single major intake in **September/October (Autumn Intake)**. Below is a detailed timeline showing the key milestones for this intake.

| Timeframe | Action Items | Key Focus |
|---|---|---|
| **Oct – Dec** | Research universities, courses, and check entry requirements. | Register for IELTS/PTE classes. |
| **Jan – Feb** | Take English proficiency test; draft SOP and collect LORs. | Aim for IELTS 6.5+ or equivalent. |
| **Feb – Mar** | Submit applications online via university portals. | Pay application fees (€35–€50). |
| **Apr – May** | Receive offer letters; apply for scholarships (GOI-IES). | Obtain Unconditional Offer. |
| **May – Jun** | Secure No Objection Certificate (NOC) and pay tuition deposit. | Send bank transfer (SWIFT) to Ireland. |
| **Jun – Jul** | Arrange financial sponsorship documents and bank balances. | Show €10,000+ for living costs. |
| **Jul – Aug** | Complete AVATS visa form; submit files at VFS Kathmandu. | Pay visa fee and submit biometrics. |
| **Aug – Sep** | Receive visa decision; purchase health insurance; book flight. | Search for accommodation. |
| **Sep – Oct** | Arrive in Ireland; register address and apply for IRP (Stamp 2). | Open Irish bank account. |

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) for Nepali Students

**Q: Can I apply for an Irish student visa with a study gap?**  
A: Yes, study gaps are accepted if they are fully explained and documented. You must provide employment letters, training certificates, or internship documents to show what you did during the gap period.

**Q: Is the Duolingo English Test (DET) accepted for Irish visas?**  
A: Yes. The Irish Immigration Service accepts Duolingo English Test (DET) scores for student visa applications, though individual universities have their own specific minimum score requirements [Source: Irish Immigration Service — https://www.irishimmigration.ie/].

**Q: What is the difference between Stamp 2 and Stamp 2A?**  
A: Stamp 2 is granted for full-time courses listed on the ILEP, allowing students to work part-time (20 hours/week). Stamp 2A is granted for courses not on the ILEP (like short term language courses), and does not allow any part-time work.

**Q: Can my spouse accompany me while I study in Ireland?**  
A: Generally, students on a Stamp 2 visa cannot bring their family members (spouse or children) to Ireland. Family reunification is only allowed for PhD students or under very specific circumstances.

**Q: Do I need a transit visa to travel to Ireland?**  
A: If your flight transits through the UK or a Schengen country, you may need a transit visa depending on your itinerary. Check transit visa requirements before booking your flight tickets.
