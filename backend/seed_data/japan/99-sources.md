# Studying in Japan — Official Sources and Portals

When preparing to study in Japan, relying on credible, official information is essential to avoid fraudulent consulting agencies and incorrect procedures. Multiple government ministries, independent administrative agencies, and embassy divisions regulate different stages of the international student lifecycle. As of June 2026, the portals listed below represent the primary, legally authoritative sources for rules regarding admissions, financing, visas, health, and work rights in Japan [Source: Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Japan — https://www.mofa.go.jp/].

---

## Japanese Government Portals (Immigration and Visas)

These portals are managed directly by Japanese ministries and are the final authority on immigration law, visa requirements, and stay extensions.

### 1. Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Japan (MOFA)
* **Website:** https://www.mofa.go.jp/
* **Role:** Sets the overall visa policies for foreign nationals entering Japan. It oversees Japanese embassies and consulates worldwide.
* **Student Utility:** Students should visit MOFA for official visa application guidelines, fee structures, and basic entry requirements.

### 2. Immigration Services Agency of Japan (ISA)
* **Website:** https://www.isa.go.jp/en/
* **Role:** An agency under the Ministry of Justice that manages the entry, residence, and departure of foreign nationals. It is the body that reviews and issues the Certificate of Eligibility (COE).
* **Student Utility:** The ISA portal provides official forms, residence card registration guidelines, rules on changing visa status (e.g., student to work visa), and the Application for Permission to Engage in Activity Other Than That Permitted (part-time work permit).

### 3. Ministry of Justice of Japan (MOJ)
* **Website:** https://www.moj.go.jp/EN/
* **Role:** Supervises the legal system, including immigration control and the registration of Japanese language schools.
* **Student Utility:** Useful for verifying the legal status and accreditation of specific Japanese language schools.

## Educational and Student Support Portals

These independent administrative bodies provide details on university enrollment, scholarships, and student welfare.

### 1. Study in Japan Portal
* **Website:** https://www.studyinjapan.go.jp/
* **Role:** A comprehensive, multi-language planning portal maintained by the Japanese government.
* **Student Utility:** Offers a search database for universities, colleges, and language schools. It provides step-by-step guides on the admission process, living costs, and preparation tips.

### 2. Japan Student Services Organization (JASSO)
* **Website:** https://www.jasso.go.jp/en/
* **Role:** An agency under the Ministry of Education, Culture, Sports, Science and Technology (MEXT) that administers student support programs.
* **Student Utility:** The definitive source for information on government scholarships (like the Monbukagakusho Honors Scholarship), university transfer credits, and the EJU exam details. JASSO also publishes annual statistical surveys on student living costs.

### 3. Ministry of Education, Culture, Sports, Science and Technology (MEXT)
* **Website:** https://www.mext.go.jp/en/
* **Role:** Sets national education standards, accredits degree programs, and funds the prestigious MEXT scholarship program.
* **Student Utility:** Explains the MEXT scholarship criteria, university rankings, and guidelines for higher education accreditation.

## Health and Labor Regulations

### 1. Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare of Japan (MHLW)
* **Website:** https://www.mhlw.go.jp/english/
* **Role:** Oversees Japan's healthcare system, labor rights, and minimum wage regulations.
* **Student Utility:** Provides information on National Health Insurance (NHI) policies and updates on regional minimum wage laws governing part-time work.

## Nepal-Specific Official Entities

### 1. Embassy of Japan in Nepal
* **Website:** https://www.np.embassy-japan.go.jp/
* **Role:** The official diplomatic mission of Japan in Kathmandu, Nepal.
* **Student Utility:** Processes visa applications, conducts interviews, and administers the selection exams for the government-sponsored MEXT scholarship program for Nepali applicants.

### 2. Ministry of Education, Science and Technology of Nepal (MoEST)
* **Website:** https://moest.gov.np/
* **Role:** The government department in Nepal overseeing academic affairs and study abroad regulations.
* **Student Utility:** Issues the mandatory No Objection Certificate (NOC) required for passport clearance at immigration and foreign currency exchange at banks.
