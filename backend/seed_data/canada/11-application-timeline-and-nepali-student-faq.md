# Canada Application Timeline and FAQ for Nepali Students

Navigating the application process for Canada requires careful planning and execution. Delays in document preparation, language testing, or visa processing can disrupt your academic plans. Having a clear timeline and understanding common student inquiries will help you manage the process successfully.

## Recommended Application Timeline

Canadian institutions typically offer three intakes: Fall (September - major intake), Winter (January - mid-sized intake), and Summer (May - limited intake). Below is a strategic timeline designed for the primary Fall intake.

*   **October to January (Research & Tests)**:
    *   Research universities, colleges, and programs.
    *   Prepare for and take English proficiency exams (IELTS Academic, PTE Academic, or TOEFL).
    *   Gather academic transcripts and certificates from SEE and NEB (or bachelor's degree).
*   **January to March (College Applications)**:
    *   Apply to your selected Designated Learning Institutions (DLIs). 
    *   Pay application fees and submit required documents (SOPs, letters of recommendation).
*   **April to May (Acceptance & Payments)**:
    *   Receive Letters of Acceptance (LOA). Choose the best option.
    *   Apply for the No Objection Certificate (NOC) from MoEST in Nepal.
    *   Pay the first-year tuition fee via SWIFT transfer using the NOC.
    *   Open a GIC account with a Canadian bank and transfer the CAD 20,635.
*   **June to July (Visa Prep & Submission)**:
    *   Receive your tuition payment receipt and GIC certificate.
    *   Complete an upfront medical exam at an approved panel physician clinic in Kathmandu.
    *   Compile financial sponsorship documents, relationship certificates, and write your visa SOP.
    *   Submit your Study Permit application online through the IRCC Portal.
*   **July to August (Biometrics & Decision)**:
    *   Receive the Biometrics Instruction Letter (BIL) and complete biometrics at VFS Global.
    *   Monitor your IRCC account for updates.
    *   Upon approval, submit your passport to VFS for visa stamping (Original Passport Request - OPR).
*   **August to September (Travel)**:
    *   Book flights, purchase health insurance, arrange accommodation, and travel to Canada. [Source: EduCanada — https://www.educanada.ca/study-in-canada-etudes-au-canada/apply-demande/before-avant.aspx?lang=eng]

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) for Nepali Students

### Q: Can I apply for a Canadian Master's degree with a 3-year bachelor's degree from Nepal?
Generally, no. Canadian universities require a four-year bachelor's degree for direct admission into a Master's program. If you have a three-year degree (such as a BA, BBS, or BSc from Tribhuvan University), you should apply for a Post-Graduate (PG) Certificate or PG Diploma program at a public college, or complete a one-year master's program or bridge course in Nepal first.

### Q: If my study permit is refused, can I apply again?
Yes. If your visa is refused, you can reapply. Before doing so, it is highly recommended to order your GCMS (Global Case Management System) notes from IRCC. These notes contain the detailed, internal remarks of the visa officer who assessed your file, revealing the exact reasons for refusal. You can then address these specific concerns in your new application.

### Q: Can I change my college or program after arriving in Canada?
Yes, you can change your institution, but you must adhere to strict IRCC guidelines. The new school must be a DLI. Once you change schools, you must log into your IRCC portal and submit a "DLI Change" notification. Failure to notify IRCC that you have changed colleges can lead to your study permit being canceled and potential deportation.

### Q: What is the total upfront cost to apply and fly to Canada?
As of June 2026, the estimated upfront cost for a Nepali student includes:
1.  First-year tuition fee: CAD 15,000 to CAD 25,000 (approx. NPR 15 to 25 Lakhs).
2.  GIC deposit: CAD 20,635 (approx. NPR 21 Lakhs).
3.  Visa fee & biometrics: CAD 235 (approx. NPR 24,000).
4.  Upfront medical exam & flights: Approx. NPR 1.5 to 2.5 Lakhs.
The total estimated cash outlay before departure ranges from NPR 38 Lakhs to NPR 50 Lakhs depending on the institution's tuition fees. [Source: IRCC — https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/study-canada/study-permit/change-dli.html]
