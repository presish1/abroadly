# Canada Study Abroad — Official Sources

To ensure the accuracy and success of your study permit application, it is vital to rely exclusively on official government channels and authorized service providers. Below is a comprehensive list of primary sources and verification portals used by international students.

## Primary Government Portals

*   **Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC)**: The official government department responsible for immigration, visas, and citizenship. This is the ultimate authority for study permits, work rights, and permanent residency rules.
    *   *Website*: https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship.html
*   **EduCanada**: The official Government of Canada portal for international students looking to study in Canada. It provides search tools for programs, tuition fee estimators, and information on scholarships.
    *   *Website*: https://www.educanada.ca/
*   **Service Canada**: The government portal responsible for issuing Social Insurance Numbers (SIN) and managing employment services.
    *   *Website*: https://www.canada.ca/en/employment-social-development/services/sin.html
[Source: IRCC — https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship.html]

## Educational and Institution Verification

*   **Designated Learning Institution (DLI) List**: Before applying to any college or university, you must verify that it is on the official DLI list. The online DLI directory specifies whether the school can host international students and if the specific program is eligible for the Post-Graduation Work Permit (PGWP).
    *   *Verification Tool*: https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/study-canada/study-permit/prepare/designated-learning-institutions-list.html
*   **Colleges and Institutes Canada (CICan)**: The national association representing public colleges, institutes, and polytechnics.
    *   *Website*: https://www.collegesinstitutes.ca/
*   **Universities Canada**: The voice of Canada's universities, providing profiles of member institutions and search engines for degrees.
    *   *Website*: https://www.univcan.ca/
[Source: EduCanada — https://www.educanada.ca/]

## Financial and GIC Institutions

*   **Guaranteed Investment Certificate (GIC) Providers**: Only specific financial institutions are approved by IRCC to offer GIC accounts for the Student Direct Stream (SDS) and regular visa routes. Approved providers include:
    *   *Scotiabank (StartRight Program)*: https://www.scotiabank.com/
    *   *CIBC (Student GIC Program)*: https://www.cibc.com/
    *   *SBI Canada Bank (Student GIC)*: https://ca.statebank.com/
*   **Nepal Ministry of Education, Science and Technology (MoEST)**: The official authority in Nepal issuing No Objection Certificates (NOC) required for international fee payments.
    *   *NOC Portal*: https://noc.moest.gov.np/
[Source: IRCC — https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/study-canada/study-permit/student-direct-stream.html]

## Medical and Biometrics Partners

*   **International Organization for Migration (IOM) Nepal**: The primary clinic for upfront medical examinations in Lazimpat, Kathmandu.
    *   *Website*: https://nepal.iom.int/
*   **Norvic International Hospital**: Approved panel physician hospital in Thapathali, Kathmandu.
    *   *Website*: https://www.norvichospital.com/
*   **VFS Global Canada (Nepal)**: The authorized visa application center in Thamel, Kathmandu, handling biometrics collection and passport transmission.
    *   *Website*: https://visa.vfsglobal.com/npl/en/can/
[Source: IRCC — https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/medical-exams/vistors-students-workers.html]

## Policy Verification and Updates

As of June 2026, immigration policies, including the cost-of-living GIC threshold and post-study work rules, are subject to change. Applicants are urged to verify all requirements on the official IRCC portal before committing funds or making travel arrangements. Never rely on unofficial blogs, forums, or unregistered consultancies to verify legal rules.
