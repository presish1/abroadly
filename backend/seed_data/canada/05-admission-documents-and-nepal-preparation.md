# Admission Documents and Nepal Preparation for Canada

Securing admission to a Canadian institution is the crucial first step. Because Canadian institutions often receive a massive volume of international applications, your documents must be clear, complete, and exactly as requested.

## Standard Admission Document Checklist

When applying to a Canadian university or college, you generally need to provide the following:

*   **Passport**: A clear, scanned copy of the bio-data page of your valid passport.
*   **Academic Transcripts and Certificates**:
    *   *For Bachelor's/Diploma*: Class 10 (SEE) and Class 12 (NEB) mark sheets and character certificates. If you are currently in Class 12, you can often apply with your Class 11 mark sheets and a "predicted grades" letter from your school for a conditional offer.
    *   *For Master's/PG Cert*: All undergraduate transcripts (semester-by-semester or year-by-year), the provisional/original degree certificate, and your Class 10/12 documents.
*   **English Language Proficiency Test Report**: A valid IELTS, TOEFL iBT, PTE Academic, or Duolingo score report (ensure the test was taken within the last two years).
*   **Statement of Purpose (SOP)**: Highly required for Master's programs and many university bachelor's programs. Less commonly required for basic college diplomas, though highly recommended. Your SOP should explain your academic history, why you chose the specific program, why Canada, and your future career plans in Nepal.
*   **Letters of Recommendation (LOR) / References**: Typically required for university applications (usually two academic references). Rarely required for college diplomas.
*   **Curriculum Vitae (CV) / Resume**: Important for postgraduate applications, especially to show any relevant work experience or to explain study gaps.
*   **Application Fee**: Non-refundable, usually ranging from CAD 90 to CAD 250 per institution.

## Important Notes on Documentation for Nepali Students

*   **Credential Assessment (WES)**: Some Canadian master's programs require you to have your Nepali degree evaluated by World Education Services (WES) Canada *before* you apply, to verify it is equivalent to a Canadian 4-year degree. Always check the specific program's requirements.
*   **Translations**: Any document not in English or French must be translated into English by an authorized translation service or a notary public in Nepal.
*   **Notarization**: Institutions usually accept scanned copies of your original documents or notarized copies. *Never* mail your original, irreplaceable NEB certificates unless explicitly instructed to do so.

## Nepal-Side Administrative Preparation

Once you receive your Letter of Acceptance (LOA), you must complete several administrative steps in Nepal.

*   **No Objection Certificate (NOC)**: To legally send money out of Nepal to pay your Canadian tuition fees, you must obtain a No Objection Certificate from the Ministry of Education, Science and Technology (MoEST) in Sanothimi, Bhaktapur. You need your LOA to apply for the NOC.
*   **Document Attestation**: The MoEST or your bank may require your academic documents to be attested by the National Examinations Board (NEB) or Tribhuvan University/Kathmandu University, and sometimes the Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MoFA), before issuing the NOC or processing the swift transfer.
*   **Opening a GIC Account**: You will need to apply online to open a Guaranteed Investment Certificate (GIC) account with a participating Canadian bank (like Scotiabank, CIBC, or SBI Canada Bank). Once approved, you will use your NOC and bank to wire the CAD 20,635 to this account.

## The Letter of Acceptance (LOA)

If your application is successful, you will receive a formal Letter of Acceptance (LOA). This is the most critical document for your study permit application.
*   It must have the institution's official letterhead, contact information, and DLI number.
*   It must state the program of study, start and end dates, and the tuition fees.
*   You cannot apply for a Canadian Study Permit without a valid LOA.
