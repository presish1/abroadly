# Admission Documents and Nepal Preparation for New Zealand

Securing admission to a New Zealand institution requires a standard set of academic and identity documents. Because of the strict visa assessment process later, it is critical that all documents submitted for admission are genuine, accurately translated (if necessary), and clearly verifiable.

## Standard Admission Document Checklist

When applying to a university or Te Pūkenga, you generally need to provide the following:

*   **Passport**: A clear, scanned copy of the bio-data page of your valid passport.
*   **Academic Transcripts and Certificates**:
    *   *For Bachelor's*: Class 10 (SEE) and Class 12 (NEB) mark sheets and character certificates.
    *   *For Master's*: All undergraduate transcripts (year-by-year or semester-by-semester), the provisional/original degree certificate, and Class 10/12 documents.
*   **English Language Proficiency Test Report**: A valid IELTS, TOEFL iBT, or PTE Academic score report (usually taken within the last two years).
*   **Statement of Purpose (SOP)**: A well-written document explaining why you chose the specific course, why New Zealand, your future career goals, and how the course aligns with your previous studies. *Note: A strong SOP here is also vital practice for the Genuine Intent statement required for the visa.*
*   **Letters of Recommendation (LOR) / References**: Typically required for postgraduate applications (one or two academic or professional references).
*   **Curriculum Vitae (CV) / Resume**: Essential for postgraduate applicants, especially if there is a gap in studies or if applying for professional programs (like an MBA).
*   **Portfolio or Audition Tape**: Only required for specific creative arts, design, or architecture programs.

## Important Notes on Documentation for Nepali Students

*   **Translations**: Any document not in English (e.g., citizenship certificates used later for visa purposes) must be translated into English by an authorized translation service or a notary public in Nepal.
*   **True Copies / Notarization**: Institutions usually require certified true copies of your original documents. This can often be done by the educational agent you are using, a notary public, or the issuing institution itself.
*   **Verification**: Be aware that New Zealand institutions and Immigration New Zealand conduct rigorous verification checks. Submitting fraudulent documents (fake mark sheets, altered IELTS scores) will result in immediate rejection, potential bans from entering New Zealand, and may affect applications to other countries.

## Nepal-Side Administrative Preparation

Before or immediately after securing your Offer of Place, you must prepare for the administrative steps required by the Government of Nepal.

*   **No Objection Certificate (NOC)**: To legally send money out of Nepal to pay your tuition fees, you must obtain a No Objection Certificate from the Ministry of Education, Science and Technology (MoEST) in Sanothimi, Bhaktapur. You will need your Offer of Place to apply for the NOC.
*   **Document Attestation**: While New Zealand universities generally accept notarized copies for admission, the MoEST or your bank may require your academic documents to be attested by the National Examinations Board (NEB) or Tribhuvan University, and sometimes the Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MoFA), before issuing the NOC or processing the swift transfer. Always check the current requirements with your bank and MoEST.

## The Offer of Place

If your application is successful, you will receive an "Offer of Place". This document will detail:
*   The course name, duration, and start date.
*   The exact tuition fees required for the first year (or the full course if less than a year).
*   Whether the offer is *Conditional* (you need to meet further requirements, like passing a final exam or an IELTS test) or *Unconditional* (you are fully accepted).

You cannot apply for a student visa or pay your fees until you have an **Unconditional Offer of Place**.
