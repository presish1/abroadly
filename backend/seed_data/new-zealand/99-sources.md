# New Zealand Study Abroad — Official Sources

To ensure the success and legality of your study journey to New Zealand, it is crucial to rely exclusively on official government platforms and authorized agencies. Below is a categorized list of primary websites and resources for verification.

## Primary Government Portals

*   **Immigration New Zealand (INZ)**: The official government agency responsible for border control, visas, and managing immigration policies. This is the ultimate authority for student visas, post-study work rights, and residency applications.
    *   *Website*: https://www.immigration.govt.nz/
*   **Study in New Zealand**: Managed by Education New Zealand (ENZ), the government agency dedicated to promoting New Zealand education globally. It offers tools for finding courses, institutions, and scholarship opportunities.
    *   *Website*: https://www.studywithnewzealand.govt.nz/
*   **Careers New Zealand**: A government service providing tools, resources, and advice on career planning and job markets in New Zealand.
    *   *Website*: https://www.careers.govt.nz/
[Source: Immigration New Zealand — https://www.immigration.govt.nz/]

## Qualifications and Quality Assurance

*   **New Zealand Qualifications Authority (NZQA)**: The government crown entity that manages the New Zealand Qualifications Framework (NZQF), registers education providers, and oversees quality assurance in the non-university tertiary sector.
    *   *Website*: https://www.nzqa.govt.nz/
*   **Te Pūkenga**: The New Zealand Institute of Skills and Technology, which unites the country's polytechnics, institutes of technology, and industry training organizations under a single national public entity.
    *   *Website*: https://tepukenga.ac.nz/
*   **Universities New Zealand**: The statutory body representing all eight of New Zealand's public universities.
    *   *Website*: https://www.universitiesnz.ac.nz/
[Source: NZQA — https://www.nzqa.govt.nz/]

## Financial and Remittance Partners

*   **ANZ Bank New Zealand (FTS Provider)**: The primary bank partnered with INZ to administer the Funds Transfer Scheme (FTS) for international students.
    *   *FTS Section*: https://www.anz.co.nz/
*   **Nepal Ministry of Education, Science and Technology (MoEST)**: The official authority in Nepal issuing the No Objection Certificate (NOC) necessary for sending fee payments to New Zealand.
    *   *NOC Portal*: https://noc.moest.gov.np/
[Source: Immigration New Zealand — https://www.immigration.govt.nz/new-zealand-visas/already-have-a-visa/managing-your-visa/transferring-funds]

## Health, Insurance, and Biometrics Partners

*   **International Organization for Migration (IOM) Nepal**: Lazyimpat, Kathmandu. The primary panel physician assessment clinic for chest X-rays and medical certificates.
    *   *Website*: https://nepal.iom.int/
*   **Norvic International Hospital**: Thapathali, Kathmandu. Authorized panel clinic in Nepal for student medical assessments.
    *   *Website*: https://www.norvichospital.com/
*   **VFS Global New Zealand (Nepal)**: The official partner in Kathmandu (Thamel) managing biometrics collection and physical passport scanning for visa processing.
    *   *Website*: https://visa.vfsglobal.com/npl/en/nzl/
[Source: Immigration New Zealand — https://www.immigration.govt.nz/new-zealand-visas/preparing-a-visa-application/medical-info/getting-a-medical-exam]

## Policy Verification and Updates

As of June 2026, immigration policies, including the NZD 20,000 living cost threshold and the 6-point Skilled Migrant Category residency system, are subject to change. Always verify requirements on official INZ and NZQA portals before making financial transfers or signing enrollment contracts.
