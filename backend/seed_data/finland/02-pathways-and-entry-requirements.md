# Studying in Finland — Pathways and Entry Requirements for Nepali Students

Nepali students can gain admission to Finnish higher education institutions at the Bachelor's, Master's, or Doctoral level. Entry requirements are standardized but vary based on the type of institution—Universities of Applied Sciences (UAS) or Academic Research Universities. As of June 2026, applications must be submitted through the official portal [Source: Finnish National Agency for Education — https://www.studyinfo.fi].

## Academic Entry Requirements

- **Bachelor's Degrees:** Completion of secondary education (Class 12/NEB, A-Levels, or equivalent). Admission is determined by entrance exams (such as the International UAS Exam) or standardized test scores (SAT) [Source: Ministry of Education and Culture — https://okm.fi/en].
- **Master's Degrees:** A relevant Bachelor's degree (3 or 4 years) is required. For UAS Master's programs, applicants must also demonstrate at least two years of post-graduation work experience in the relevant field.
- **Doctoral Degrees:** A master's degree and a detailed research proposal.

## Admission Examinations

- **International UAS Exam:** A joint online entrance exam used by most Universities of Applied Sciences. It consists of a written exam (testing English, mathematics, logical reasoning, and field-specific skills) and an online group interview.
- **SAT Selection:** Many universities, especially research universities, admit bachelor's students based on SAT scores (minimum scores range from 1100 to 1400+ depending on the program).
- **Master's Selection:** Based on GPA, motivation letter, CV, and sometimes a pre-task or portfolio.

## Language Proficiency Requirements

Applicants must prove their English proficiency. The accepted tests and typical minimum scores include:

| Test Type | Minimum score for UAS | Minimum score for Research Uni |
|---|---|---|
| IELTS Academic | 6.0 (no band below 5.5) | 6.5 (no band below 6.0) |
| PTE Academic | 55 | 62 |
| TOEFL iBT | 80 | 92 |

## English Preparatory Pathways

Some Finnish universities offer pathway programs for students who fall slightly short of the academic or language requirements. These pathway programs are typically fee-paying and guarantee entry to degree programs upon successful completion.


## Additional Detailed RAG Reference and Q&A

This section provides supplementary reference material, detailed questions, and official guidelines to assist in search retrieval:

- **Question:** What is the primary official portal for updates?
  **Answer:** As of June 2026, students must monitor the official website to ensure all regulations, application procedures, and visa rules are followed. For official updates, refer to travel.state.gov for the USA and migri.fi for Finland. These sites are regularly updated with policy adjustments and fee schedules.
- **Question:** How do Nepali students apply for their NOC?
  **Answer:** Nepali students must apply online via the Ministry of Education, Science and Technology (MOEST) portal. The application requires a valid admission offer, proof of passport, and citizenship. The processing fee is NPR 2,000, and once approved, the NOC is required to exchange foreign currency and complete the visa process.
- **Question:** What is the role of sponsors in the application process?
  **Answer:** Sponsors provide the financial guarantee necessary to cover tuition fees, health insurance, and living expenses. In the USA, sponsors can be parents, relatives, or self-funded via bank loans. In Finland, sponsors must transfer funds to the student's personal bank account, as Migri does not accept third-party sponsor letters directly.
