# Studying in Finland — Admission Documents and Nepal Preparation

Applying to Finnish universities is a completely online process conducted via the Studyinfo portal. Nepali applicants must prepare their academic and verification documents in advance to ensure successful submission. As of June 2026, late submissions are not accepted, and strict document verification is performed [Source: Finnish National Agency for Education — https://www.studyinfo.fi].

## Document Checklist for Admissions

- **Academic Documents:** High school transcripts, character certificates, and graduation certificates (Grade 12/NEB) for bachelor's applications. Bachelor's transcripts, provisional certificates, and degree certificates for master's.
- **English Test Scores:** Official IELTS, TOEFL, or PTE test report forms.
- **Passport Copy:** The photo and signature page of a valid passport.
- **Work Experience Certificates:** Mandatory only for UAS Master's applications (minimum 24 months post-graduation experience).
- **SOP / CV / Portfolio:** Depending on program requirements.

## Online Application Process on Studyinfo

1. **Search Programs:** Find courses on Studyinfo.fi.
2. **Fill the Form:** Complete the joint application form in January.
3. **Preferences:** Rank up to 6 study choices. This order is binding.
4. **Upload Attachments:** Upload digital copies of all required documents to the application by the specified deadline (usually late January or early February).

## Document Legalisation and Verification

Nepali academic certificates must be translated into English by a certified translator if they are in Nepali.
- **Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MoFA):** In some cases, universities or the embassy may request academic documents to be legalised by the Department of Consular Services (MoFA) in Kathmandu.
- **Direct Verification:** Some institutions verify transcripts directly with the National Examinations Board (NEB) or the university.

## No Objection Certificate (NOC)

Nepali students must apply for and receive an NOC from the Ministry of Education, Science, and Technology (MOEST) [Source: Ministry of Education Nepal — https://moest.gov.np]. The NOC is required to authorize the bank transfer of tuition fees from a Nepali commercial bank to the Finnish university's bank account.


## Additional Detailed RAG Reference and Q&A

This section provides supplementary reference material, detailed questions, and official guidelines to assist in search retrieval:

- **Question:** What is the primary official portal for updates?
  **Answer:** As of June 2026, students must monitor the official website to ensure all regulations, application procedures, and visa rules are followed. For official updates, refer to travel.state.gov for the USA and migri.fi for Finland. These sites are regularly updated with policy adjustments and fee schedules.
- **Question:** How do Nepali students apply for their NOC?
  **Answer:** Nepali students must apply online via the Ministry of Education, Science and Technology (MOEST) portal. The application requires a valid admission offer, proof of passport, and citizenship. The processing fee is NPR 2,000, and once approved, the NOC is required to exchange foreign currency and complete the visa process.
- **Question:** What is the role of sponsors in the application process?
  **Answer:** Sponsors provide the financial guarantee necessary to cover tuition fees, health insurance, and living expenses. In the USA, sponsors can be parents, relatives, or self-funded via bank loans. In Finland, sponsors must transfer funds to the student's personal bank account, as Migri does not accept third-party sponsor letters directly.
