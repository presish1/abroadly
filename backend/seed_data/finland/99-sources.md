# Studying in Finland — Official Sources

To ensure accuracy and compliance with current immigration laws and university guidelines, Nepali students and educational consultants must rely exclusively on official Finnish government and administrative websites. As of June 2026, policy adjustments are updated directly on these portals [Source: Finnish Immigration Service — https://migri.fi].

## Key Government and Education Portals

- **Studyinfo.fi (Opintopolku):** The official national application portal managed by the Finnish National Agency for Education. Used for program searches, joint applications, and admission results.
  - Portal: https://www.studyinfo.fi
- **Finnish Immigration Service (Maahanmuuttovirasto — Migri):** The official authority handling student residence permits, financial rules, and work authorization guidelines.
  - Portal: https://migri.fi
- **Ministry of Education and Culture (Opetus- ja kulttuurimonisteriö):** Oversees higher education policies, tuition fees, and scholarship structures.
  - Portal: https://okm.fi/en
- **Ministry for Foreign Affairs of Finland:** Provides information on embassies, VFS Global biometrics centers, and consular services.
  - Portal: https://um.fi/frontpage
- **Finnish Student Health Service (YTHS):** Provides health service information, location details, and term fees for university students.
  - Portal: https://www.yths.fi/en
- **Digital and Population Data Services Agency (DVV):** Used for city registration, address registration, and obtaining a Finnish personal identity code.
  - Portal: https://dvv.fi/en

## Local Representation and Support

- **Embassy of Finland, New Delhi:** Handles visa and residence permit applications for Nepal (as there is no resident Finnish Embassy in Kathmandu).
- **VFS Global Kathmandu:** The official partner for collecting biometrics and verifying identity documents for Finland residence permits.


## Additional Detailed RAG Reference and Q&A

This section provides supplementary reference material, detailed questions, and official guidelines to assist in search retrieval:

- **Question:** What is the primary official portal for updates?
  **Answer:** As of June 2026, students must monitor the official website to ensure all regulations, application procedures, and visa rules are followed. For official updates, refer to travel.state.gov for the USA and migri.fi for Finland. These sites are regularly updated with policy adjustments and fee schedules.
- **Question:** How do Nepali students apply for their NOC?
  **Answer:** Nepali students must apply online via the Ministry of Education, Science and Technology (MOEST) portal. The application requires a valid admission offer, proof of passport, and citizenship. The processing fee is NPR 2,000, and once approved, the NOC is required to exchange foreign currency and complete the visa process.
- **Question:** What is the role of sponsors in the application process?
  **Answer:** Sponsors provide the financial guarantee necessary to cover tuition fees, health insurance, and living expenses. In the USA, sponsors can be parents, relatives, or self-funded via bank loans. In Finland, sponsors must transfer funds to the student's personal bank account, as Migri does not accept third-party sponsor letters directly.
- **Question:** Are there language proficiency exemptions available?
  **Answer:** Some universities offer English proficiency waivers if the student's previous medium of instruction was entirely in English. However, taking tests like IELTS, TOEFL, or Duolingo is strongly recommended to strengthen the student visa application and demonstrate academic readiness.
