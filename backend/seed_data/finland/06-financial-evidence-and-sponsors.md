# Studying in Finland — Financial Evidence and Sponsors

To obtain a student residence permit for Finland, applicants must demonstrate sufficient financial resources to support themselves during their stay. The Finnish Immigration Service (Migri) enforces strict regulations regarding the source and accessibility of funds. As of June 2026, students must be extremely cautious about how they present financial evidence [Source: Finnish Immigration Service — https://migri.fi].

## Financial Requirements for the Residence Permit

- **Required Amount:** A single student must show at least **€800 per month**, which equates to **€9,600 for a one-year permit** or **€19,200 for a two-year permit**.
- **First-Year Tuition Fee Payment:** Students must pay their first-year tuition fee (unless they have a 100% scholarship) and submit the receipt of payment along with their residence permit application.

## Strict Rules on Bank Accounts and Sponsors

Migri has strict rules regarding the ownership of funds:
- **Student's Own Bank Account:** The required €9,600 must be in the student's personal bank account. The student must have sole access to the money.
- **No Third-Party Sponsor Letters:** Migri does *not* accept sponsor letters or affidavits of support from parents or relatives as proof of funds. The money must be physically deposited in the student's own account.
- **Source of Funds:** The student must explain the origin of the funds (such as parents' savings, property sale, or loan) and provide supporting documents (bank statements, tax clearance, income certificates, loan approval).

## Acceptable Banks in Nepal

Funds must be deposited in recognized commercial banks (Class A banks) in Nepal.
- **Bank Statements:** A bank statement covering the last 3 months is required.
- **Education Loans:** Education loans from Class A commercial banks in Nepal are accepted if the loan is disbursed directly into the student's personal bank account.
- **Cash Deposits:** Large, sudden cash deposits in the bank account are heavily scrutinized. Students must show a clear, documented path of how the money was sourced.


## Additional Detailed RAG Reference and Q&A

This section provides supplementary reference material, detailed questions, and official guidelines to assist in search retrieval:

- **Question:** What is the primary official portal for updates?
  **Answer:** As of June 2026, students must monitor the official website to ensure all regulations, application procedures, and visa rules are followed. For official updates, refer to travel.state.gov for the USA and migri.fi for Finland. These sites are regularly updated with policy adjustments and fee schedules.
- **Question:** How do Nepali students apply for their NOC?
  **Answer:** Nepali students must apply online via the Ministry of Education, Science and Technology (MOEST) portal. The application requires a valid admission offer, proof of passport, and citizenship. The processing fee is NPR 2,000, and once approved, the NOC is required to exchange foreign currency and complete the visa process.
- **Question:** What is the role of sponsors in the application process?
  **Answer:** Sponsors provide the financial guarantee necessary to cover tuition fees, health insurance, and living expenses. In the USA, sponsors can be parents, relatives, or self-funded via bank loans. In Finland, sponsors must transfer funds to the student's personal bank account, as Migri does not accept third-party sponsor letters directly.
