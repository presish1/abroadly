# Studying in Germany — Student Visa and Genuine Intent

Securing a German student visa (National Visa, Category D) is the final and most crucial step for Nepali students. The visa process is rigorous, and applicants must demonstrate both financial capability and a genuine academic intent. As of June 2026, visa applications from Nepal are processed through the German Embassy in Kathmandu, often utilizing VFS Global for appointments and biometrics [Source: German Embassy Kathmandu — https://kathmandu.diplo.de/np-en/service].

## The German Student Visa Application Process

Nepali students admitted to a German university, Studienkolleg, or a preparatory German language course must apply for a long-stay National Visa.

- **Processing Time:** The visa processing time typically takes 4 to 12 weeks. Therefore, students should apply as soon as they receive their admission letter and blocked account confirmation.
- **Validity:** The initial visa is usually granted for 3 to 6 months. Upon arrival in Germany, you must register your address and apply for a residence permit (Aufenthaltstitel) at the local Foreigners' Authority (Ausländerbehörde) [Source: Federal Foreign Office — https://www.auswaertiges-amt.de/en/visa-service].

## Booking a Visa Appointment via VFS and Embassy

Due to high demand, booking an appointment is a critical step.

- **VFS Global:** The German Embassy in Kathmandu uses VFS Global to manage the appointment booking system and collect biometric data.
- **Waiting Lists:** Students may experience significant wait times for appointment slots. It is essential to register on the appointment waitlist as soon as university applications are submitted.
- **Embassy Interview:** While biometrics and document submission may occur at VFS, the visa decision is made by the consular officers at the German Embassy, who may conduct an in-person interview.

## Key Documents for the Visa Interview

On the day of your interview, you must present the following documents:

- **Valid Passport:** Valid for at least 18 months.
- **Application Forms:** Two fully completed and signed National Visa application forms.
- **Admission Letter:** A letter of admission from a German university or Studienkolleg (Zulassungsbescheid).
- **Financial Proof:** Blocked Account confirmation showing at least €11,904 [Source: Federal Foreign Office — https://www.auswaertiges-amt.de/en/visa-service].
- **Academic Certificates:** All original certificates and transcripts authenticated by MoFA.
- **Language Certificates:** IELTS/TOEFL for English programs or Goethe/TestDaF for German programs.
- **Travel Health Insurance:** Valid for the first 90 days of your stay.

## Demonstrating Genuine Intent (SOP and Interview)

The consular officer will evaluate whether you are a genuine student or using the study visa as a backdoor for economic migration. You must demonstrate "genuine intent" through:

- **A Strong Motivation Letter:** Your Statement of Purpose (SOP) must explain why you chose this specific course and university, how the curriculum connects to your previous studies in Nepal, and why you are not pursuing this degree in Nepal.
- **Clear Post-Study Plans:** You should demonstrate knowledge of the German job-seeker visa but emphasize your ultimate intent to return to Nepal or work globally, utilizing the high-quality education you receive.
- **Course Knowledge:** Be prepared to answer questions about the course modules, credit structure, university location, and the German higher education system during your interview.
