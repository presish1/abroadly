# Studying in Germany — Institutions, Courses, and Intakes for Nepali Students

Germany has over 400 higher education institutions offering more than 20,000 study programs. The system is diverse and includes universities, universities of applied sciences, and art/music academies [Source: DAAD — https://www.daad.de/en/studying-in-germany/].

## Types of Institutions

**Universitäten (Universities):** Research-focused institutions offering bachelor's, master's, and PhD programs. Strong in theoretical and academic subjects. Examples: TU Munich, RWTH Aachen, Heidelberg University, LMU Munich, Humboldt University Berlin.

**Fachhochschulen (Universities of Applied Sciences / HAW):** Practice-oriented institutions with strong industry links. Programs include mandatory internships. Examples: Frankfurt University of Applied Sciences, Hochschule München. Ideal for students wanting hands-on training.

**Kunst- und Musikhochschulen (Art and Music Academies):** Specialised institutions for fine arts, design, music, and performing arts. Admission typically requires a portfolio or audition.

## How to Search for Courses

- **DAAD International Programmes Database:** The primary tool for finding English-taught programs — https://www2.daad.de/deutschland/studienangebote/international-programmes/en/. Filter by degree level, subject, language, and tuition fees.
- **Hochschulkompass:** Comprehensive database of all study programs in Germany (including German-taught) — https://www.hochschulkompass.de/en.html.
- **Individual university websites:** Always verify program details, admission requirements, and deadlines on the specific university's page.

## Intake Months

| Semester | Classes Start | Application Deadline |
|---|---|---|
| Winter Semester (Wintersemester) | October | 15 July (for most programs via uni-assist) |
| Summer Semester (Sommersemester) | April | 15 January (fewer programs available) |

**Important:** Most bachelor's programs start only in the winter semester. Many master's programs also have only winter intake. Some competitive programs have earlier deadlines — check each program individually [Source: uni-assist — https://www.uni-assist.de/en/].

## Popular Fields for Nepali Students

Based on enrollment trends, Nepali students in Germany commonly pursue:

- **Engineering:** Mechanical, automotive, electrical, civil, industrial engineering
- **Computer Science and IT:** Informatik, data science, artificial intelligence, software engineering
- **Business and Economics:** MBA, business administration, international management, economics
- **Natural Sciences:** Physics, chemistry, biology, environmental science
- **Public Health and Life Sciences:** Public health, biomedical engineering, biotechnology

## English-Taught Programs

As of June 2026, over 2,000 programs are taught entirely in English, predominantly at the master's level. English-taught bachelor's programs exist but are fewer (around 200) and often at private universities or select public institutions [Source: DAAD — https://www2.daad.de/deutschland/studienangebote/international-programmes/en/].

## uni-assist Application Processing

Many German universities use **uni-assist** as a centralised application service for international students. uni-assist evaluates your foreign credentials against German standards before forwarding your application to the university [Source: uni-assist — https://www.uni-assist.de/en/].

- **Fee:** ~€75 for the first application, €30 for each additional application to other uni-assist member universities.
- **Timeline:** Submit applications 6–8 weeks before the university deadline to allow processing time.
- **VPD (Vorprüfungsdokumentation):** Some universities require a VPD from uni-assist instead of a full application through them.
