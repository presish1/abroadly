# Studying in Germany — Official Sources and Useful Links

Having access to authentic and up-to-date information is essential when planning your studies in Germany. Nepali students should avoid relying on unverified forum posts or third-party consultancy claims. As of June 2026, the official portals listed below represent the primary, legally binding sources for academic admission, document recognition, visa requirements, and immigration policies [Source: German Embassy Kathmandu — https://kathmandu.diplo.de/np-en/service].

## Key Government and Diplomatic Portals

- **German Embassy Kathmandu:** The official diplomatic mission representing Germany in Nepal. It is the sole authority for issuing student visas and legalizing official documents for Nepali citizens.
  - *Link:* https://kathmandu.diplo.de/np-en/
- **Federal Foreign Office (Auswärtiges Amt):** The German ministry responsible for foreign relations and visa policies. It provides global rules for blocked accounts, Schengen visas, and national study visas.
  - *Link:* https://www.auswaertiges-amt.de/en/
- **Federal Ministry of the Interior and Community (BMI):** The government body overseeing residence rights, naturalization laws, and the Skilled Immigration Act.
  - *Link:* https://www.bmi.bund.de/EN/

## Study Research and Course Databases

- **DAAD (Deutscher Akademischer Austauschdienst):** The German Academic Exchange Service is the largest funding organisation for international students. Its website hosts the comprehensive International Programmes database, scholarship search tools, and step-by-step guides.
  - *Link:* https://www.daad.de/en/
  - *Course Search:* https://www2.daad.de/deutschland/studienangebote/international-programmes/en/
- **Research in Germany:** A portal funded by the Federal Ministry of Education and Research, offering comprehensive information for PhD candidates and researchers.
  - *Link:* https://www.research-in-germany.org/en/

## Application and Document Evaluation Portals

- **uni-assist e.V.:** The service association for international student applications. It evaluates the equivalence of school and university certificates for over 180 German universities.
  - *Link:* https://www.uni-assist.de/en/
- **Anabin (KMK):** The central database for the evaluation of foreign educational certificates. It allows students to check if their Nepali high school (NEB) or university degree is recognized in Germany.
  - *Link:* https://anabin.kmk.org/
- **Studienkolleg Association:** The official portal containing details about preparatory courses, Feststellungsprüfung (FSP) sample papers, and contact info for all state Studienkollegs.
  - *Link:* https://www.studienkollegs.de/

## Blocked Account and Health Insurance Providers

- **Expatrio Portal:** Government-approved digital provider for student blocked accounts and health insurance packages.
  - *Link:* https://www.expatrio.com/
- **Fintiba Portal:** Digital provider offering online Sperrkonto setup and incoming travel insurance.
  - *Link:* https://www.fintiba.com/
- **Techniker Krankenkasse (TK):** One of Germany's largest statutory public health insurance providers for international students.
  - *Link:* https://www.tk.de/en/
