# Studying in South Korea — Application Timeline and Nepali Student FAQ

Starting studies in South Korea requires careful adherence to institutional timelines and embassy protocols. Documents must be collected, legalized, and submitted months in advance. Additionally, Nepali students commonly have questions regarding visa regulations, dependent visas, study gaps, and changing fields of study. As of June 2026, the application procedures and timeline reflect the latest guidelines from the embassy and university admission boards [Source: Embassy of the Republic of Korea in Nepal — https://overseas.mofa.go.kr/np-en/index.do].

---

## Recommended Application Timeline for March Intake (Spring)

The Spring semester (starting in March) is the main intake for South Korean universities. Here is the ideal timeline starting 8 months prior:

* **July – August (Previous Year):** Research universities and select courses. Begin intensive Korean language study (aim for TOPIK exam registration). Start the document legalization chain (Notary → Education Board/University Registrar → MoFA Nepal attestation).
* **September – October (Previous Year):** Universities release their admission guidelines. Submit online applications and pay the application fee. Mail the physical, consularly confirmed document package directly to the university in Korea.
* **November – December (Previous Year):** Universities conduct document reviews and video interviews (often via Zoom or Skype). Admission decisions are announced by mid-December.
* **January (Intake Year):** Apply for the No Objection Certificate (NOC) from MoEST Nepal. Pay the tuition fee (typically for 1 semester or 1 year) via bank wire transfer (Telegraphic Transfer - TT). The university sends the physical Certificate of Admission (CoA).
* **Late January – Early February (Intake Year):** Get the Tuberculosis (TB) Health Certificate from an embassy-designated hospital. Book a visa slot and submit the visa application at the Embassy of South Korea in Kathmandu.
* **Mid-February (Intake Year):** The visa is processed and stamped (usually takes 7 to 15 days). Book flight tickets and attend pre-departure orientations.
* **Late February (Intake Year):** Fly to South Korea. Move into the dormitory, attend university orientation, and register your address [Source: National Institute for International Education — https://www.studyinkorea.go.kr/].

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) for Nepali Students

### Q1: Is a study gap acceptable for admission and visa?
* **Answer:** Yes, moderate study gaps are acceptable. A gap of 1 to 3 years after Class 12 or Bachelor's is easily approved if the student has been working or taking training courses. Gaps of more than 3 years face high scrutiny for language courses (D-4-1), but are acceptable for graduate programs (D-2-3) with solid work experience in the same field.

### Q2: Can I bring my spouse with me to South Korea?
* **Answer:** Language students (D-4-1) are **not permitted** to bring dependents. However, students enrolled in regular degree programs (D-2) can sponsor their spouse and children on an **F-3 Dependent Visa**. The student must show they have the financial capability (bank funds and housing) to support their dependent without relying on part-time work.

### Q3: What happens if my visa application is rejected by the embassy?
* **Answer:** If a visa is rejected, the student will receive a rejection slip stating the general reason (e.g., lack of financial stability, unclear study purpose, or lack of language ability). The student can re-apply in the next intake after fixing the specific document discrepancies. The tuition fee paid to the university is refundable, minus a small admin fee, upon presenting the visa rejection letter.

### Q4: Can I change my major or transfer universities in Korea?
* **Answer:** Yes, but it is complex. You can change your major or transfer to another university after completing your Korean language course (D-4) or after completing your first year of a degree course (D-2), subject to university approval. You must notify immigration and update your ARC details within 14 days of the transfer [Source: Korea Visa Portal — https://www.visa.go.kr/].
