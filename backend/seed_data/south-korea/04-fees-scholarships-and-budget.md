# Studying in South Korea — Fees, Scholarships, and Budgeting

Financial planning is a cornerstone of a successful study experience in South Korea. Nepali students must understand the costs of tuition, the types of scholarships available to offset these costs, and the practical day-to-day living expenses in South Korean cities. As of June 2026, the South Korean government continues to regulate university tuition increases while expanding local scholarship programs to remain a highly competitive study destination [Source: National Institute for International Education — https://www.studyinkorea.go.kr/].

---

## Tuition Fees by Program and Institution

Tuition is charged per term for language courses and per semester (two semesters per academic year) for degree programs.

### 1. Korean Language Programs (EoHakWon)
* **Cost per Term (10 weeks):** KRW 1,200,000 to KRW 1,800,000 (approx. NPR 120,000 to NPR 180,000).
* **Annual Cost (4 terms):** KRW 4,800,000 to KRW 7,200,000 (approx. NPR 480,000 to NPR 720,000).
* **Payment:** Most universities require students to pay for at least two terms (20 weeks) upfront to issue the documents needed for a visa.

### 2. Associate and Undergraduate Degrees (Semester Fees)
* **National and Public Universities:** KRW 1,800,000 to KRW 3,500,000 per semester (approx. NPR 180,000 to NPR 350,000).
* **Private Universities:** KRW 3,000,000 to KRW 6,500,000 per semester (approx. NPR 300,000 to NPR 650,000).
* **Junior Colleges:** KRW 2,200,000 to KRW 3,800,000 per semester.
* *Note: Medical, dental, and engineering programs are significantly more expensive than humanities or social science majors [Source: Ministry of Education of the Republic of Korea — https://english.moe.go.kr/].*

### 3. Graduate Degrees (Master's and PhD)
* **National Universities:** KRW 2,500,000 to KRW 4,500,000 per semester.
* **Private Universities:** KRW 4,000,000 to KRW 8,000,000+ per semester.

## Scholarship Opportunities

To attract international talent, South Korea offers excellent scholarship options:

### 1. Global Korea Scholarship (GKS)
Managed by the National Institute for International Education (NIIED), the GKS is a fully-funded government scholarship:
* **Coverage:** Full tuition fees, round-trip airfare, a monthly stipend (KRW 1,000,000 for undergraduate; KRW 1,000,000 to KRW 1,500,000 for graduate), settlement allowance, medical insurance, and a 1-year Korean language training grant.
* **Application:** Two pathways: Embassy track (via the Korean Embassy in Kathmandu) and University track [Source: Embassy of the Republic of Korea in Nepal — https://overseas.mofa.go.kr/np-en/index.do].

### 2. University-Specific TOPIK Scholarships
Most private universities offer automatic tuition discount schemes for international students based on their TOPIK scores at entry:
* **TOPIK Level 3:** 30% to 40% tuition waiver for the first semester.
* **TOPIK Level 4:** 40% to 50% tuition waiver.
* **TOPIK Level 5 or 6:** 70% to 100% tuition waiver.
* *Subsequent Semesters:* The scholarship is maintained based on the student's GPA in the preceding semester.

### 3. Brain Korea 21 (BK21)
A government-backed research scholarship program for graduate students in science, technology, and engineering, providing monthly research stipends.

## Monthly Budgeting and Cost of Living

Living costs depend heavily on the location (Seoul vs. provincial cities) and the choice of accommodation:

* **Rent (Accommodation):**
  * **On-Campus Dormitory:** KRW 200,000 to KRW 400,000 per month (safest and most economical).
  * **Goshiwon / Goshitel:** KRW 250,000 to KRW 450,000 per month. These are tiny, single-room units with no deposit requirement, often including free rice and kimchi.
  * **One-Room (Studio Apartment):** KRW 400,000 to KRW 700,000 per month. Requires a high deposit (Key Money / Wolse deposit) ranging from KRW 3,000,000 to KRW 10,000,000+.
* **Food:** KRW 300,000 to KRW 500,000 per month (eating at university cafeterias costs KRW 4,000 - 6,000 per meal).
* **Transportation & Utilities:** KRW 150,000 per month.
* **National Health Insurance:** Approx. KRW 75,000 per month.
* **Total Estimated Budget:** KRW 800,000 to KRW 1,400,000 per month (approx. NPR 80,000 to NPR 140,000).
