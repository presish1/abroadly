# Studying in South Korea — Official Sources and Portals

Navigating the administrative requirements for studying in South Korea requires using verified, official resources. Multiple South Korean government ministries, independent administrative agencies, and diplomatic missions regulate the academic, financial, and immigration aspects of the student experience. As of June 2026, the portals listed below are the authoritative channels for information regarding admissions, scholarships, visas, labor rights, and health insurance in South Korea [Source: Ministry of Education of the Republic of Korea — https://english.moe.go.kr/].

---

## South Korean Government Portals (Immigration and Visas)

These portals are managed by ministries responsible for national security, immigration control, and legal residency status.

### 1. Korea Visa Portal
* **Website:** https://www.visa.go.kr/
* **Role:** The official visa information and application tracking system managed by the Ministry of Justice.
* **Student Utility:** Students use this portal to check visa requirements, download visa application forms, track the status of their visa applications, and verify the issuance of electronic visas or visa numbers.

### 2. Ministry of Justice of South Korea (MOJ)
* **Website:** https://www.moj.go.kr/EN/
* **Role:** Oversees immigration policies, long-term stay categories, part-time work permit regulations, and residence registration (ARC).
* **Student Utility:** Provides legal updates on changes to immigration laws, points tables for D-10/F-2-7 visas, and regulations for change of visa status.

## Educational and Scholarship Portals

These entities are dedicated to managing higher education standards and administering government scholarships.

### 1. Study in Korea Portal
* **Website:** https://www.studyinkorea.go.kr/
* **Role:** The official government-funded planning portal managed by the National Institute for International Education (NIIED), a branch of the Ministry of Education.
* **Student Utility:** Provides a database of all accredited Korean universities and language schools, online application portals for select universities, detailed scholarship announcements (such as the GKS), and guides on living costs and accommodation.

### 2. National Institute for International Education (NIIED)
* **Website:** https://www.niied.go.kr/eng/
* **Role:** Administers the Global Korea Scholarship (GKS) and holds the Test of Proficiency in Korean (TOPIK).
* **Student Utility:** The definitive source for TOPIK exam schedules, GKS eligibility criteria, and statistics on international students in Korea.

### 3. Ministry of Education of South Korea (MOE)
* **Website:** https://english.moe.go.kr/
* **Role:** Sets national education standards, regulates university tuition, and accredits junior colleges and universities.
* **Student Utility:** Useful for checking university accreditation status and research rankings.

## Health and Labor Portals

### 1. Ministry of Health and Welfare of South Korea (MOHW)
* **Website:** https://www.mohw.go.kr/eng/
* **Role:** Governs the National Health Insurance (NHI) system that covers international students.
* **Student Utility:** Provides updates on health insurance policy details, premium costs, and coverage rules.

### 2. Ministry of Employment and Labor of South Korea (MOEL)
* **Website:** https://www.moel.go.kr/english/
* **Role:** Oversees labor standards, workplace safety, and minimum wage policies.
* **Student Utility:** Governs the minimum wage rates for part-time student work (arubaito) and protects students from labor exploitation.

## Nepal-Specific Official Entities

### 1. Embassy of the Republic of Korea in Nepal
* **Website:** https://overseas.mofa.go.kr/np-en/index.do
* **Role:** The diplomatic mission of South Korea in Kathmandu, Nepal.
* **Student Utility:** Handles visa submissions, conducts interviews, and manages consular confirmations for Nepalese academic transcripts.

### 2. Ministry of Education, Science and Technology of Nepal (MoEST)
* **Website:** https://moest.gov.np/
* **Role:** Issues the mandatory No Objection Certificate (NOC) required for foreign currency transfers and airport clearance in Nepal.
