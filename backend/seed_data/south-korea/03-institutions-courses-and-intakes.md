# Studying in South Korea — Institutions, Courses, and Intakes

Nepali students can choose from a range of academic and vocational institutions in South Korea, each designed to serve different educational and professional needs. The South Korean academic calendar operates on distinct semester cycles, and applications must be prepared well in advance of these intakes. As of June 2026, the South Korean Ministry of Education has increased support for vocational junior colleges to encourage international students to enter hands-on fields [Source: Ministry of Education of the Republic of Korea — https://english.moe.go.kr/].

---

## Types of Higher Education Institutions

South Korea’s tertiary education sector comprises several institutional structures:

### 1. 4-Year Universities (Daehakgyo)
Universities offer bachelor’s, master’s, and doctoral degrees. They are categorized into:
* **National Universities:** Funded and operated by the national government (e.g., Seoul National University, Pusan National University, Kyungpook National University). They offer low tuition and focus heavily on research.
* **Private Universities:** Funded by private educational foundations (e.g., Yonsei University, Korea University, Hanyang University, Sungkyunkwan University). They comprise the majority of institutions in Korea and feature modern facilities and highly specialized majors.

### 2. Junior Colleges (Jeonmun Daehak)
Junior colleges offer 2-to-3-year vocational programs granting associate degrees.
* **Focus:** Practical training and job-ready skills (e.g., cosmetic science, beauty art, culinary arts, nursing care, mechanical engineering, smart agriculture).
* **Work Connection:** Programs are designed in collaboration with industries, facilitating internships and direct hiring upon graduation.

### 3. Graduate Schools (Daehakwon)
Focus exclusively on Master’s, PhD, and postdoctoral research. They are divided into academic graduate schools (general research) and professional graduate schools (business, international studies, law).

### 4. Korean Language Institutes (EoHakWon)
Most universities operate their own language training centers. They offer intensive Korean programs divided into 6 levels (Level 1 beginner to Level 6 advanced) to prepare students for degree courses.

## Academic Semester Intakes and Deadlines

The academic year in South Korea begins in the spring, which is different from the fall start common in Western countries.

### 1. Spring Semester (Primary Intake)
* **Start Month:** Early March.
* **Application Window:** Typically between August and October of the previous year.
* **Target Students:** Main intake for all undergraduate, associate, and graduate degree programs.

### 2. Fall Semester (Secondary Intake)
* **Start Month:** Early September.
* **Application Window:** Typically between February and April of the same year.
* **Target Students:** Very popular for graduate students and English-medium undergraduate programs.

### 3. Korean Language Program Intakes (D-4 Visa)
Language institutes run on quarterly terms with four separate intakes:
* **Terms:** Spring (March), Summer (June), Fall (September), and Winter (December).
* **Application Deadlines:** Generally 3 months prior to the start of the term (e.g., apply by December for the March term) [Source: National Institute for International Education — https://www.studyinkorea.go.kr/].

## Popular Courses and Majors

* **Engineering & Technology:** Mechanical, electronic, chemical, and software engineering. These programs are closely linked to Korea's major corporate research labs.
* **K-Beauty, Fashion & Cosmetic Science:** Korea is a global hub for beauty, cosmetics, and styling. Junior colleges offer popular, hands-on associate degrees in beauty design and skincare technology.
* **Business Administration & MBA:** High-ranking business schools offer English-medium BBA and MBA programs with global internships.
* **Information Technology & AI:** Covers cybersecurity, data science, and artificial intelligence, benefiting from Korea's leading IT infrastructure.
* **Hospitality and Culinary Arts:** Practical training in hotel management and international cuisine with high placement rates in local hotels and resorts.
