# Studying in South Korea — Pathways and Entry Requirements

Nepali students can access South Korean higher education through different pathways based on their linguistic ability and academic history. The South Korean admissions process is highly structured, and meeting academic and language benchmarks is mandatory for university acceptance and visa issuance. As of June 2026, immigration and education departments require verified language test scores to ensure students can follow their chosen curricula [Source: National Institute for International Education — https://www.studyinkorea.go.kr/].

---

## Pathway 1: The Korean Language Program Pathway (D-4-1 to D-2)

This is the most common entry route for Nepali students. Since most degree courses are conducted in Korean, students first improve their language skills in Korea:

* **Structure:** Students enroll in a university-run Korean Language Institute under a **D-4-1 General Student Visa**.
* **Duration:** Typically 6 months to 1 year (can extend up to 2 years). Programs consist of four 10-week terms per year.
* **Progression:** Once the student achieves the required **TOPIK (Test of Proficiency in Korean) Level 3 or 4**, they can apply for a **D-2 Regular Student Visa** to begin their undergraduate or associate degree program.

---

## Pathway 2: Direct Entry to English-Medium Programs (D-2)

Many major South Korean universities offer English-taught programs, especially in global business, international relations, engineering, and graduate studies:

* **Structure:** Students apply directly from Nepal and enter on a **D-2 Visa**.
* **Language Prerequisite:** Standardized English tests (IELTS, TOEFL) are required.
* **Advantage:** Allows immediate entry into degree courses without spending a preparatory year studying Korean. However, basic Korean skills must still be developed for daily living and employment.

---

## Pathway 3: Direct Entry to Korean-Medium Programs (D-2)

Students who have already learned Korean in Nepal and passed the official TOPIK exam can apply directly for degree programs:

* **Structure:** Direct admission to a bachelor's, master's, or PhD program under a **D-2 Visa**.
* **Language Prerequisite:** Must have a minimum of **TOPIK Level 3** (for science/engineering) or **TOPIK Level 4** (for humanities/business).

---

## Academic Entry Requirements

The academic requirements vary depending on the level of study:

* **Associate Degree (Junior Colleges - 2 to 3 years):** Requires completion of 12 years of formal education (Class 12 / NEB or equivalent). A minimum GPA of 2.2 out of 4.0 (50%+) is usually required.
* **Bachelor's Degree (Universities - 4 years):** Requires completion of 12 years of formal education. GPA requirements are competitive: national/top-tier universities require a GPA of 3.0+ out of 4.0 (70%+), while provincial private universities may accept GPAs from 2.4+.
* **Master's and Doctoral Degrees (Graduate Schools - 2+ years):** Master's applicants must hold a recognized Bachelor's degree (minimum 3 or 4 years), and PhD applicants must hold a Master's degree. GPA equivalent to 70%+ is standard, along with a detailed research proposal and academic references [Source: Ministry of Education of the Republic of Korea — https://english.moe.go.kr/].

## Language Proficiency Requirements Summary

| Program Type | Required Korean Score | Required English Score |
|---|---|---|
| Korean Language (D-4-1) | No official minimum (basic familiarity preferred) | N/A |
| Associate/Bachelor's (Korean-taught) | TOPIK Level 3 or 4 | N/A |
| Bachelor's (English-taught) | N/A | IELTS 5.5 - 6.5 or TOEFL iBT 80+ |
| Graduate Programs (Korean-taught) | TOPIK Level 4 or 5 | N/A |
| Graduate Programs (English-taught) | N/A | IELTS 6.0 - 7.0 or TOEFL iBT 80-90+ |

*Note: For graduation, most Korean universities require students in Korean-taught majors to achieve TOPIK Level 4 before they can receive their degrees [Source: National Institute for International Education — https://www.studyinkorea.go.kr/].*
